# Frog illustrations

Watercolour illustrations made for Soundscape Atlas, cut out of their white
background by `scripts/cut_out_illustration.py`.

| File | Species |
|---|---|
| assa-darlingtoni.webp | Pouched Frog (*Assa darlingtoni*) |
| crinia-signifera.webp | Common Eastern Froglet (*Crinia signifera*) |
| dryopsophus-pearsonianus.webp | Cascade Tree Frog (*Dryopsophus pearsonianus*) |
